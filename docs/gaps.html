Where CADCraft falls short of AutoCAD

Last reviewed: 2026-10-10 · Last updated: 2026-10-25 · Change: minor (Named Viewports dialog: U11 narrowed; MVIEW Restore and PURGE of multiline styles had landed: F15 and F21 corrected) · Target: Autodesk AutoCAD 2027

Every known shortfall, one row each, ranked within each section by user impact. This is the work list: agents pick from here (top of a section first), and remove or update a row in the same PR that closes it. Numbers and weights live in target-app-parity.md; hours are Opus 5.5 agent-hours, calibrated as explained there. "Est." means a judgement from the cited evidence. Rows marked [alpha blocker] are what fails the alpha gate; do them first.
